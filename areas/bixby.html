Bixby Plumbing, Block by Block

Bixby straddles the Arkansas River, and river-corridor soils move — that shifting stresses slab foundations and the sewer laterals running under them. Out toward the edges of town, some properties sit on septic or have made the septic-to-sewer transition, which brings its own set of headaches. Closer in, it's the familiar Tulsa-metro mix: hard-water sediment in water heaters and aging supply lines. Our pros diagnose first, quote upfront, and show up in a real arrival window.
