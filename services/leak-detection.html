Slab Leaks and Tulsa's Clay Soil

A huge share of Tulsa homes sit on concrete slabs poured over expansive clay. When Oklahoma's wet springs and dry summers make that soil heave and settle, the copper or PEX lines running through the slab flex — and eventually pinhole or crack. The leak runs silently for weeks: your only clues are the water bill, a warm patch of floor, or the faint sound of running water at 2 a.m. Catching it early is the difference between a targeted repair and major foundation and flooring damage. If any of those signs sound familiar, don't wait.
