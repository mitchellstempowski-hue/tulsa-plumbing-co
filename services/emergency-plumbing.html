Do this now

While You Wait for the Plumber

Shut off the water

Find your main shutoff (usually near the meter or where the line enters the house) and turn it clockwise. This stops the damage from spreading.

Kill power to affected areas

If water is near outlets, appliances, or the breaker panel, switch off those breakers — water and electricity don't mix.

Contain and document

Towels and buckets buy time. Snap photos of the damage for your insurance claim.
